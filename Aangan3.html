Walk through your home

Drag anywhere to look around. Walk with the round pad at the bottom left, or with the arrow keys or W A S D on a keyboard.

Tap a room name to go straight there, tap the map to walk to a spot, or play the guided tour and watch.

Open Light and fans to change the time of day and the brightness, and to switch the lights and fans in each room. On a keyboard, L switches the light and F the fan in the room you are in.
